Abstract

A customer-lead time-series dataset, available at \url{URL} is presented with time and quality of different acquired leads along with many client-side and technical-side features that are suspected to affect outcome quality. We analyze the data in three stages: First, we analyze the change of lead quality through time. Second, we narrow down on the factors that affect lead quality most significantly through statistical criteria and models. Finally, we propose a scheme of growth for the advertiser to generate more and better leads.

Preprocessing

We prescribe some ideas as to how to handle different variables in our analysis process. We also recode a few (mostly into integers) for ease of analysis, interpretation and communication.

  1. LeadCreated: Kept intact.
  2. FirstName: Dropped. (Note: Labelling generic or spammy names to predict lead quality could be a useful future enhancement.)
  3. Email: Kept intact. Interactions can be grouped by email, assuming an entity consistently uses the same email.
  4. VendorLeadID: Kept intact to group multiple interaction events together.
  5. CallStatus: Recoded into a new variable called CallStatusNo as follows (where higher scores indicate greater “closeness” to a closed lead):
    • NaN: Kept intact.
    • Unable to contact - Bad Contact Information: 1 (Lowest rung of quality; unusable lead).
    • Contacted - Invalid Profile: 2 (Nearly hopeless, unless prospective customers decline with false excuses).
    • Contacted - Doesn't Qualify: 3 (Some potential for future interest).
    • EP Sent: 4
    • EP Received: 5
    • EP Confirmed: 6
    • Closed: 7 (Highest level of success).
  6. WidgetName: First 3 common segments removed. The next 3 segments are split and expanded into new columns: FormPgsCnt, Fieldset, and ColorDesign.
  7. PublisherZoneName: First segment (common display-size info) removed. The second segment (position) is recoded into a binary variable (0/1) since it only has 2 alternatives.
  8. PublisherCampaignName: Recoded into a binary variable (2 alternatives).
  9. AddressScore: Missing values imputed via linear regression using other variables. Serves as a predictor for CallStatusNo, as higher scores reflect consumer sincerity.
  10. PhoneScore: Missing values imputed via linear regression using other variables. Serves as a predictor for CallStatusNo, as higher scores reflect consumer sincerity.
  11. AdvertiserCampaignName: Recoded into a binary variable.
  12. State: Kept intact.
  13. Debt level: Converted into an ordinal variable ranging from 1 to 7. (An alternative approach using class means can be considered, carefully handling the right open-ended final class).
  14. IP Address: Dropped. (Filtering spammy or malevolent IPs can be tested in advanced stages).
  15. Partner: Kept intact.
  16. Rest of the columns (ReferralDomain, MarketingCampaign, AdGroup, Keyword, SearchQuery, Referral URL, ReferralURL Parameters, LandingPageURL, LandingPageURL parameters): Dropped for the time being.
